
> [!Background]
> Kol Djurk is a young male Goblin who began life on the cold edge of a trading road,
> abandoned and left to make way for faster beasts and less curious travelers. He was 
> found, thin and wary, by an apothecary owner Tom and his patient wife Marigold who
> took him in more out of habit than heroism — they needed an extra pair of hands for
> drying roots and running errands — and then, over time, out of a quiet, stubborn
> affection. In the cramped backroom of their shop Kol learned the names of plants and
> the alchemy of poultices, how to tell a healable wound from one that needed a surgeon,
> and which bitter teas could settle a goblin stomach. He kept a habit of pocketing bright
> seeds and folding scraps of his foster parent's worn maps into his collar, small rebellions
> that were forgiven for the many ways he made their house laugh.
>    
>    One morning, while gathering moonwort and foxglove deeper in the woods than he was
> told to go, Kol misjudged some brush and stepped on a hunters snare which left him
> dangling, broken and cold; a passing adventurer—a broad-shouldered adventurer with a
> laugh like thunder—cut him free, bandaged his leg with a scrap of cloak, and fed him
> stew. The adventurer's stories of vast caverns, talking ruins, and daring rescues lit
> something in Kol that Tom’s lamp never did.
>  
>    Now Kol dreams of swapping his mortar and pestle for a short, sharp blade, of healing
> friends in the midst of danger, and of mapping every hollow he's only skirted in
> whispers. Small and sudden, clever with fumes, and with a surprising steadiness in his
> hands, Kol fancies himself useful on a road where quick wits matter as much as muscle.
> He intends to repay the one who saved him by saving others, and he is determined to
> become an adventurer.

WHY HE WAS CHOSEN: His Bravery

DEATH:
- Weeks after that life changing moment, Kol was out in the woods, once again collecting herbs, when he over heard a commotion up head. Carefully making his way forward, he found a few  highwaymen threatening an elderly couple travelling on the road. The old man was shakily holding out a sword, desperately trying to protect his wife. The bandits toyed with the elderly couple, but when the old man scored a hit on one of the bandits they turned murderous. 
- Kol could not stand by and watch, he drew his small herb reaping sickle and before the lead bandit could strike down the old man he summoned a strength he did not know he possessed and cut the hand off the lead bandit. 
- In retaliation, the other two surround him, and though he fought his best, they cut him down. As the lift slipped from his eyes, however, he saw the his former savior, the unnamed hero, ride up to drive the bandits off and finish the job Kol started. Kol, had helped save the elderly couple. 
- As he passed, the hero whispered a prayer over Kol, before all went dark.
