Aka: Sol Cinder

> [!Background]
> Sol's power was just beginning to emerge, when a plague known as "Ashen Fever" spread through his genasi village. Desperate to find a way to save his family, he pushed the limits of his newly found power, collapsing into unconsciousness from the effort, days later he awoke to eerie silence across his home. The fever had stolen everyone he ever loved, but his power had saved him. He is now scarred with the memories of losing his parents and loved ones, and Sol being powerless to help. Now he wrestles with this new power and hopes of finding a way to preserve life and not let anyone else feel the sorrow of loss.

WHY HE WAS CHOSEN: His Desire to Preserve Life
