
# Act II - The Dark Druid

## Chapter 6 - The Fens of Fallon 
**The Lightning Nuts set out towards the Fens of Fallon, beginning their pursuit of the Dark Druid, and the first day ended with the group fording a river and setting team camp on the far bank. As evening descended, a few opened up about what the Earthmother showed them in the Sacred Grove. Most confirmed that they saw their own death, but uncharacteristically Al-Dahr was the most vocal and forthcoming. The usually reserved firbolg, described seeing the Dark Druid destroy his village before stabbing him in through the back. Kol, mentioned "trying to help" someone, while Solomon asked if anyone knew of the Summer Queen. Sol mentioned thinking she might be his patron and was somehow involved in the memory of his death. Lastly Fink didn't mention any specifcs, while Éamon ignored everyone and refused to talk about what he saw.**

**That evening, while taking his watch, Éamon disappeared in the woods, hunting for something. Getting lost, he stumbled upon a cave den full for hippogriffs, but was uninterested in the beasts. Instead, he returned to camp, ignoring questions from Fink who was taking over the watch. The rest of the evening passed uneventfully and in the morning the group arose to a delicious breakfast cooked by Kol, before continuing on their quest. During their travels through the foothills, they met and appeased a hungry troll, before arriving at the border of the Fallon woods. Avoiding the woods, the group skirted the lake shore and by evening reached the Fens of Fallon proper. Setting camp on the dry forest edge, Éamon once again went hunting, got lost, and returned to camp frustrated. During the evening Fink noticed a city in the middle of the lake, bathed in moonlight, while Kol noticed some small bobbing lights far off in the Fens. Despite the oddities, the group were undisturbed and in the morning set out into the Fens.**

**Navigating the marsh was slow and hazardous and past midday the marsh turned into a fetid bog. As the day waned further, a thick noxious fog began to descend over the bog, making Fink sick. Soon the group found themselves face to face with a strange bullywug bog sage who named himself "Lord of the Muddy Pool" and demanded a toll to pass the bog. Éamon stepped towards the "Lord" and soon after insulted him and began a deadly battle with the bullywug and his knights. Fortunately for the group, a knight of questionable allegiance seized on the opportunity to claim the title of Lord with the previous bog sage was slain. This new Lord was more amenable to allowing the group to pass and was happy with his new promotion among his peers. Reaching the far shore, the group set camp again for the night, and this time while Éamon tried to hunt he soon found himself lost in the fog and stepped in a deep mud pit, having to pull himself free. During the night, Sol heard some strange wooden chimes and whispering, but again the group were not assaulted during the night.**

**Setting out the next morning, the party travelled north, following Éamon who went to the lake to wash off the mud from his previous evening's mishap, before heading west into the woods. The farther they travelled, the denser and darker the woods became. Continuing their travels, they soon found their way blocked by a n unusual [maskling](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6755205-maskling-seer) creature that was calling aggressively in an unknown whispery language before attacking them, joined by their maskling allies. After the battle, the group found little in the way of clues to who the creatures were, but they did find some simple weapons, masks, small vials of paint, and Éamon found a small shrine with a charm made of twigs hanging from it. Sol was able to identify it as a [charm of plant command](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/602337-charm-of-plant-command) and Éamon gave it to Kol, who seemed excited by the item.**

**As evening approached, the woods thinned again and soon opened up to a large expanse, ruined by corruption. The Lightning Nuts set camp near the shore, and saw (and smelled) a fish kill floating down the coast from the source of the corruption. Fink also noticed the island city in the middle of the lake again, and discerned it is the capital city of the LeShay, Karador. In the morning as they crossed the ruined landscape, they soon found themselves on the edge of the corrupted woods, and found an abandoned maskling village up in the trees. As the marched deeper, the corruption worsened, the air becoming sickening. More worrisome, was the way the corrupted vines seemed to part before them, leading the way, and the number of blights standing stock still in the woods around them, watching but not attacking. The final unsettling encounter was a firbolg, unknown to Al-Dahr, who seemed taken by the corruption and was unresponsively eating black oozing mushrooms.**

**Time passed strangely in the corrupted woods, and after hours or days, the Lightning Nuts finally found themselves before a large moonwell grove, turned black with corruption. Standing, before the moonwell was the Dark Druid, who welcomed them and offered them a worthy death, being the first sacrifices to connect the moonwell to the realm of Kazgoroth, Gahenna. He also, explained that this moonwell, in particular, was chosen due to its history. I was the site of Kazgoroth's last awakening and despite being defeated, when his still beating heart was cut out by the Sword of Cymrych Hugh it sank to the bottom of the well decaying a permanently corrupting the once White Well, into what is now known as the Darkwell.**

**As the Lightning Nuts went to attack the Dark Druid, he began raising blights, including a massive [tree blight](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195237-tree-blight) to impede their progress. Those that attacked the Druid directly found themselves overwhelmed by a corrupted aura surrounding the Druid along with taking the brunt of his powerful magic. After multiple members of the party were knocked unconscious, needing restorative healing, it became apparent that they were outmatched by the Dark Druid's power and in the moment when all looked lost, the astral form of the Steward of the Grove arrived, temporarily holding the Druid and opening a small portal in the well for the party to escape. Solomon, Éamon and Fink grabbed their companions and entered the well.**

## Chapter 7 - The Dark Between
**What they found on the other side of the portal was a pitch black land. The ground was made of obsidian glass and a dark grey "sun" hung in the sky providing little light. Éamon lit his moonblade and Fink cast faerie fire, in the hopes of providing light, but soon the magical light began to draw strange raven-like creatures made of shadow. When the "ravens" began feeding on the light, they felt it best to extinguish the light, and instead tend to Kol and Al-Dahr's wounds in the dark. After an hour or so, the two began to stir back to life and the party set out in the dark expanse, feeling it was better to move towards the unknown then to stay in place. When they finally decided to stop for the "night", Éamon once again lit his moonblade, but instead of drawing the ravens, this time they drew the attention of some malevolent shadows. The party quickly disposed of the shadows, and returned to the darkness, just in case more dangerous menaces lay about.**

**That night as Fink was on watch, he sensed a faint pulsing beacon far off in the distance pulling him towards it. Luckily it was in relatively the same direction they had already been travelling, and so in the morning the party set out, following Fink's lead. As the day drew on, however, fatigue from the lack of water began to wear some of the Lightning Nuts, slowing their progress. The next day passed much the same, a slow trudge through the darkness, following the pulse, but without water, Fink began imbibing his other (more potent) sources of liquid and soon found it tougher to navigate the party, who were sliding further into an exhausted state. During the "day" Sol had tested casting magical fire to see if it would draw anymore shadows, but nothing appeared, until later that evening when Éamon activated his moonblade again. This time the light drew juvenile shadow dragons, providing a tougher but still manageable challenge. One of the dragons, however, managed to escape and fade back into shadow, continuing to taunt the watchers that night, but did not try to attack the party again.**

**As the march continued, a now sober Fink could feel the pulsing getting slightly stronger, but without water the party was beginning to fatigue further. Finally, Al-Dahr came to the rescue, creating an alchemical jug that could create 12-gallons of water, including other liquids like ale (or even mayonnaise!). Hydrated the party began to quickly feel better and after another couple days of travel, finally managed to reach their destination, a 5 foot orb glowing with moonlight. Two lines of light extended off into the distance, but a third broken light of line c pointed in the direction they came from. The party had wisely tied themselves together to provide security in the darkness, and as Kol reached to touch the orb, that connection allow Fink to sense and then open a gate to a new moonwell.**

## Chapter 8 - The Moray Isle
**Opening the gate, the Lightning Nuts found themselves transported to a new moonwell in an unfamiliar woods. The moonwell was swirling with power as they passed through and then slowly settled down again to a tranquil pool. A soak and sip in the pool fully refreshed the last of the fatigue from the Lightning Nuts, and restored they set out through the woods to find out where they were. As they emerged from the woods, most saw an equally unfamiliar plain sloping down towards a river far off in the distance, Éamon, however, saw the plains that surrounded his home and began moving quickly in the direction of his town. The others followed, but Éamon refused to answer their questions as he led on. Approaching the town, they found the way barred by large dire wolves, and Éamon viscously sprang to the attack, but did not hesitate to keep moving towards the town as soon as the wolves were slain. When the town came clearly into view, the destruction and ruin was evident. The town was nothing but an empty charred remains of its former self.**

**Entering the city, Éamon moved purposefully towards the small castle, perched atop a hill. The others kept close to the swift moving Éamon, but scanned the ruined town warily. As they neared the base of the hill, Kol noticed a large funeral pyre deeper in the city, but the need to follow their companion kept the party for inspecting further. As they reached the top of the hill and entered the castle gates, they found the remains of a small single funeral pyre amidst the wreckage of the castle courtyard. Éamon, searching the ashes, found a wolf's head ring and finally shed some light on his need to reach the castle. He told the others that his was his home town and that we was raised within the walls of this castle. The town, Dynnegall was constantly set upon by werewolves, and it was his father's duty as the Lord of Dynnegall to protect the town from that threat. The ashes are that of his father, who fell during the devastation of the town, and the ring he found is a honor only given to those who have slain a werewolf. He then urged the others to keep an eye out for his father's weapon, a halberd shaped like a wolf's head.**

**From there, Éamon led them in to the castle and found it ruined. Furniture lay broken, banners torn or shredded by claws, and valuable looted. Éamon ascended the stone stairs, skipping the second floor, and heading towards his room on the third. Al-Dahr and Fink followed, but Kol remained on the bottom floor to explore the Great Hall, while Sol stopped to inspect the rooms on the second. Kol found a kitchen in the back of the Great Hall, and although it had been ransacked, he was able to find some fresh food supplies with a careful search. Sol first entered a small chapel with a shrine to the Earthmother, before entering a larger room that acted as both an armory and a war room. Here he found two large maps, one of the Moray Isle and the other of the entire Moonshae Isles. After searching scattered documents he was able to piece out that the ffolk controlled only the northern half of Moray Isle. The lower half is under the control of werewolves known as the Black Blood Tribe under the viscous leadership of Argon Bael. The Moonshae Isles as a whole has been divided as well. The ffolk, led by King Derid Kendrick, ruling much of the isle, but in addition to the LeShay controlled Gwynneth Isle, the northwest isles are ruled by the the Northlanders under the rule of King Brandon Olafsson.**

**On the third floor, Éamon entered his old room, Fink following, while Al-Dahr entered a small sunroom and began mending a broken stained glass window. Éamon's room, like the rest of the castle, was a disaster, but despite the sacking it was easy to see his life centered around training with martial weapons. Éamon began searching the room for something, finally stopping when he found a small book beneath the ruins of a table. Book in hand, he then asked Fink to go inspect the funeral pyre in the town below, looking for anymore of the wolf's head rings. Fink quickly flew off, gathering Kol in the process, to do as his distraught friend requested. Meanwhile, Éamon ventured to the top room, his parent's. There he sought out the family's hidden wall safe, and unlocking it, found the funds for the care of the town, his mother's emerald necklace and his family's Writ of Authority over Dynnegall signed by King Derid Kendrick. As he descended from the top room, he found Al-Dahr had finished repairing the stained glass and told the firbolg that the depiction of the man wielding a sword above his head and the female druid by his side are depictions of King Tristan Kendrick and his queen the Great Druid Robyn Kendrick. They lived centuries ago, but were the first rulers to unify the entire Moonshae Isles.**

**Heading down togehter, Éamon and Al-Dahr found Sol in the armory, the walls now bare of the maps by the fire genasi's efforts to remove them. As they departed the castle, Fink and Kol returned with two more rings and a report that they saw signs of someone hiding in the ruined town. The group went together and soon found a small street urchin boy, Emrys, who survived the raid by hiding in a latrine. While he didn't see much of the battle, he did tell that about a week ago the other survivor's lit the funeral pyres for the slain townsfolk and the Lord, before heading north for the safety of Caer Moray, and that they were being led by a gruff one-armed warrior. Hearing this the Lightning Nuts rested for the evening and then in the morning headed north. After a days' travel they reach the high stone-walled city of Caer Moray.**
