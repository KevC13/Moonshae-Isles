# Act I - The Corrupted Moonwells

## Chapter 1 - The Earthmother's Request 
**The story begins with five characters awaking in a cavern with a pool of still, nearly clear, water in the center. The pool gives off a faint glow of silvery-blue light where the moonlight reflects off the glassy surface. The five have no memory their past or how they arrived in the cavern, but still know who they are. The spend the first few minutes getting to know each other before exploring their surroundings. They are;**

**Al-Dahr = A nearly 10 foot tall grey skinned Firbolg, who initially ignores the group, content to roll back over and goes back to sleep.** 

**Éamon = An athletic black haired Half-Elf intent on saving others.**

**Fink = A gruff chain smoking winged tiny humanoid (definitely not a Fairy) initially seen urinating in the pool.**

**Kol = A young green skinned, golden eyed Goblin who is very excited to be here.**

**Solomon "Sol" = A quiet smoldering Fire Genasi with black hair and thin lines of fire etched into his ash grey skin.**

**Exploring the cavern, the five found two dark paths leading out of opposite sides of the central room. Most were able to see capably in the dark, save Al-Dahr, but the small Kol took the Firbolg's hand a led him through the spiraling paths. At the end of the first path the found a dead end, but when Éamon inspected the ground he found a round wooden symbol with a red rose surrounded by green vines. The symbol had a small leather chord attached, to allow it to be worn as a necklace. Éamon gave it to Kol, and the young goblin excitedly adorned it. At the end of the other tunnel, they found a small tone shrine with the same symbol engraved into it. Éamon, taking a moment to think on the symbol, recognized it as the symbol of the Earthmother, the goddess of nature and balance on the Moonshae Isles. He also determined that the pool in the center of the cavern is a "moonwell", a source of power and connection between the Earthmother and the land of Moonshae.** 

**Returning to the moonwell, the party decided to take a swim, while Fink flew through the ceiling to assess their surroundings. Above, Fink found a small clearing in a grove of trees, allowing the full moon light to shine on the moonwell. Studying the pool, Fink noticed it was perfect circular and discerned it was magical in nature, providing healing, energy and sustenance. He also realized that there should be a third path to the north of the well and when he went to inspect the cave wall, discovered there was an illusory wall hiding a new path forward. At the end of this path, the five found a stairwell leading up, and Éamon lifted the stone slab at the top to reveal a stone circle surrounded in a grove of trees. At the edge of the stone circle, sitting near a fire, sat a cloaked woman who greeted them and introduced herself as the "Steward of the Grove."** 

**She went on to explain that this is a sacred site of power known as a moonwell dedicated to the goddess of nature, the Earthmother. The Earthmother brought each of them here, through the power of the Moonwell, to aid her in her time of need. Throughout the land, the Moonwells are being corrupted, weakening the Earthmother and endangering all of Moonshae. The Steward was asked to greet you, to bring you some needed items and to point you in the proper direction, but she guesses that the Earthmother sees some good in you, with some important role to play, but that you will need to find your own strength before you can succeed.** 

**Before explaining more, the Steward (aka Stew), directed them to some needed supplies. Each grabbing travel packs, weapons, armor, and tools of their choice. After gathering their gear, the Steward then explained that the Earthmother has retreated to The Sacred Grove, located at the center of this island, Sarifal, as a precaution. Sarifal is the core of her connection to the rest of the isles, and the Earthmother asks that you cleanse two other nearby Moonwells, one at Kingsbay and one at Darkhorn Castle, to stabilize her power. After doing so, the Earthmother will be safe to open The Sacred Grove and give you answers.**

**To cleanse the Moonwells,  you will need a relic imbued with the power of the moon. One such relic is hidden beneath the altar of an old Earthmother temple in the nearby ruined city of Caer Corwell. The Steward warned though that the Unseelie Fey and malicious beasts have inhabited the ruined city since the fall of the Ffolk. She advised to stick to the edges of the city, not to linger, and not to go to the Caer Corwell Castle.** 

**She suggested going to the small port town of Kingsbay after retrieving the relic. It will offer some respite and a chance to buy anything you may need. It is mainly inhabited by Ffolk, but all kinds are welcome. From there you will be able to cleanse the Moonwell, located in the nearby wood.**

**Her final warning was to beware crossing the mountains into the northwest part of the isle, known as the Myrloch Vale. Since the return and reign of High Lady Ordalf most of Sarifal has been claimed for the leShay (high fey beings). Other elves and fey creatures have sworn their allegiance to the High Lady and both groups are not fond of outsiders, particularly humans. Any travels in their lands will need to carry the High Lady's approval.**

**After that she invited you to sleep, promising a safe rest. During the night each of you have dreams of your past, the power of the grove repairing some of what was lost. You still did not remember the last few weeks, but you learned the details of where you came from and the skills you trained.**

**In the morning, the Steward called Sol over, sensing his innate magic, and advised that untrained innate power is both a blessing and a curse. He is not shackled by the rules or preconceptions of years of study, but there are may also temptations. She warned to beware of trading your morals or favors for quick access to power. Some beings offer power at a cost, others offer it freely.** 

**As you set out to leave she mentioned that " these woods are sacred and protected by me alone. If you have need of me, you will find a small standing stone with the Earthmother's symbol when you exit the forest. Return there and make an earnest prayer to the Earthmother and I will come to your aid." She then conjured a small fawn made of green light, saying "Show them the way little one". As the fawn approaches the dense trees, a path emerged, and the fawn continued down the path, leading the way out of the grove.** 

## Chapter 2 - The Temple of Caer Corwell
**You emerged from the forest beside a small two foot high standing stone with the rose pattern of the Earthmother carved into it. As the fawn stepped from the forest it disappeared and the path behind you closed, leaving you looking out upon an open vale that stretched east as far as the eye can see. To the west the vale ended in a small port city nestled between a high rocky cliff and a large cove. Atop the high cliff was a large castle, and the city below but was in ruins. Near where the vale meets the city wall you saw a large round temple like structure.**

**Éamon "lead" the party along the mountain wall that borders the city, until you neared the temple. From there the five of you marched towards the east side of the temple upon arriving all but Sol entered. Inside you found a brilliant white stone, mithral, and moonstone are all used for elaborate decorations. The center of the roof was open to the sky, beneath which was a small marble altar sitting at the base of a 20 foot tall marble statue depicting the Earthmother as an elderly woman adorned in clothes of flowing vines, leaves and flowers. Around the rest of the rotunda were carved various images related to the moon, from moon flowers to owls and symbols of the moon in all its phases.**

**After some careful direction from Fink, Kol discovered a small square tile at the base of the altar that he was able to pry free with his sickle, below which was a small amulet intricately crafted from mithral. A crescent moon wrapped around a large, luminescent moonstone that had a brilliant white shine to it. Kol gave the Moon Amulet to Éamon, who immediately donned it and studying it realized it was magical and required attuning to learn more.** 

**While the others explored the temple, Sol stood guard outside and soon heard voices and oily footsteps approaching. Sol immediately warned the others, but in their slow retreat they were discovered by two oil black creatures with overly large heads and hands known as [boggles](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17224-boggle). Al-Dahr unsure of what to do, lobbed his knife towards the creatures and then tried to trap one in a bucket, while Kol, Fink and Éamon drew weapons and attacked. Fink, the most seasoned of the fighters proved the most capable, Éamon eventually landed a couple hits and aided in taking out the creatures. During the battle however, Al-Dahr took a serious blow and Kol ran to his aid. As the little goblin worked to heal him, his hands began to channel magical healing for the first time and he was able to restore him fully.** 

**Wary of more enemies nearby, you five left the temple, heading out the same way you entered. You then set your focus on traveling towards Kingsbay, and the first of the moonwells you need to cleanse. Éamon (aka Kol) led the group, quickly finding the old road that ran between Caer Corwell and Kingsbay. After a few hours hiking on the road, you took a short rest during which Éamon was able to attune to the Moon Amulet, learning its power. Soon after resuming their travels, Solomon caught wind of a group following the party, and you soon learned it was an unseelie pixie and four goblins following from Caer Corwell. While most of you debated what to do, some wanting to fight, others wanting to hide or run, Fink flew towards the group, meeting the pixie midway. Fink then expertly persuaded/deceived that you five were not responsible for the mayhem in the temple and that they unseelie group should return to the Caer Corwell to try and find the true culprits.** 

**After that encounter, the rest of the journey that day was uneventful. As evening drew near, you all stopped to make camp and rest. Realizing you were out of water, most went to search for water, while Sol stayed to start a fire. You found a nearby small pond, using Al-Dahr's oil soaked bucket to boil the water (except the iron gutted Fink who drank it immediately to no ill effect). After munching on some rations, you all fell into a restful sleep, taking turns to watch over the camp.**

**The night passed uneventfully in the outside world, but you awoke to the sound of Kol shouting excitedly that he would accept. Solomon was the last to awake and Fink and Kol noticed he did so appearing to have grasped someone hand along with faint flash of green passing along his fingertips. While Kol cooked up some breakfast, which Al Dahr hungrily consumed, you soon learned that each of you had dreamed more of your past. After sharing, some more than others, you guys set out along the road once again, but soon found the trail overgrown and had to navigate the terrain more slowly. The march proved more difficult than the previous day, but as dusk approached, you saw signs of the road returning and soon stumbled upon a small village.**

**Entering the village you found it long abandoned, and while Kol and Al-Dahr worked on pulling water out from the town well, Éamon deftly climbed atop one of the still standing buildings to look for signs of danger. While up there, he noticed a few wolves moving out in the grasslands nearby. Although they did not pose an immediate threat, you set another watch just to be safe. That evening during Sol's watch, the wolves made their way into town, sniffing around the back of the house you were occupying. In Sol's nervousness, he tripped over Kol, stirring the little goblin, who took action screaming loudly and brandishing his sickle. The wolves seeing this fled in terror.**

**The rest of the evening passed quietly and you once again set out towards Kingsbay along the now returned road. Towards midday, Solomon heard danger ahead and you soon found yourselves facing five bandits, each bearing a scar in the shape of an upside down triangle with a slash through it. Fink immediately became enraged and darted forward, lopping the head clean off one of the bandits. The bandit leader was able to keep the rest of his party from fleeing, and they foolishly pressed the attack. The fight was over in mere moments, Éamon killed the leader, Kol convinced two others to change their ways as they fled, and Al-Dahr bound another in magically created manacles.**

**Questioning the prisoner, you learned that they bandits were Outcasts, the triangular scar a marking them for all to see. They had been caught trespassing on LeShay lands and were marked for it. Now they were forced to live as bandits to survive. No town would admit them for fear of angering the LeShay. Kol suggested the man seek the moonwell in Corwell Grove to see if he could be healed, but while Solomon searched the man's bag he found a cloth with the mark of Bhaal, the god of death, on it. Fink intimidated the man and further learned that a cult of Bhaal had taken up residence in Darkhorn Castle and was hiring the bandits to help them seek out moonwells, whishing to corrupt them. Hearing this, Éamon stabbed the man through the heart. With nothing left to learn, you set off again and soon crested a hill to see the town of Kingsbay nestle along the coast.**

## Chapter 3 - Kingsbay
**Descending into port town of Kingsbay, you decided you needed an adventuring group name, and "somehow" landed on the name the Lightning Nuts. More confident in you band you introduced yourselves to a guard who told of a town market for purchasing items and the hospitable Silver Salmon Inn for food, drink, and rest. You inquired about the Lord of Kingsbay and he mentioned that the Lord Aaron MacFeagus holds daily meetings each morning with anyone in town who would like to raise a concern.**

**With evening approaching, you decided to head towards the Silver Salmon Inn and there found a large cozy gathering area with warm fires, good food, and good drinks. The owner, Maeve Thorne, had an overall friendly demeanor, but had a vocal distaste for nobles and was not a fan of Éamon. Ushering Éamon to a seat, you were able to get some information from Maeve regarding troubles in the area. While she did not know where the moonwell was, she did tell of black waters and dead fish to the south near the Kingsbay Woods, and missing boats and ill omens from the north around Darkhorn Castle. After gleaning what knowledge you could from her, you finished your food, arranged for some rooms and mostly went to bed. Éamon sipped some wine until late in the evening, and Al-Dahr decided to stay up all night tinkering.**

**In the morning, an exhausted Al-Dahr showed off the fruits of his labor, sporting googles shaped like owls eyes that allow him to see in the dark and a set of magical maps that he gave to Kol, Sol, and Fink showing each of them the location of the others. From there you hit the town market to stock up on rations and other mundane supplies. Kol, however, specifically sought out an old herbalist woman to stock up on healing supplies, while Éamon sought a blacksmith to get a crossbow. Feeling refreshed and stocked up, you made your way towards the Lord's Manor, to offer your aid to Lord Aaron MacFeagus.**

**When you first arrived, Éamon haughtily interrupted a local speaking with Lord Aaron, thinking his noble birth gave him the right to do so. Lord Aaron, however, quickly put him in his place, telling him to mind his manners. Éamon did not take the hint, and soon found himself being escorted/carried from the manor house by two house guards. When it was your turn to speak with Lord Aaron, no one was eager to step forward, until finally the small goblin healer, Kol, introduced himself and explained that the Lightning Nuts were sent by the Earthmother to help cleanse the moonwells. Hearing this, Lord Aaron was visibly relieved and was able to give you specific directions to the Kingsbay moonwell. He alse warned that all was not well in the woods. In addition to the black tides emanating from the waters near the woods, dark creatures have been seen lurking, the animals have become more aggressive, and the several scouts he has sent to investigate have never returned. In exchange for you help, he will offer a hefty amount of gold and he will cover your lodging at the Silver Salmon Inn anytime you are in the town.**

**No wanting to waste the light, you left Kingsbay and made your way towards the woods. At first the path was easy to follow, but as you travelled deeper the woods began to darken and decay. Approaching a rotten smelling stream you were ambushed by blighted creatures in the form of animated twigs, needles and vines. While you were victorious over the dark creatures, it was not without pain. In particular Kol, Fink, and Éamon bore the brunt of the attacks by the blights and required aid from Al-Dahr to be healed. Tending to your wounds, you began pushing deeper into the now darkened woods, seeking the source of the darkness, the corrupted moonwell.**

**Moving deeper into the woods, the signs of the corruption worsened. Black sickening fog, massive decay and rot, and infected animals all surrounded the corrupted moonwell grove. When the Lightning Nuts were finally able to breakthrough into the grove, they found the moonwell an oily black pool. Around the pool, the bodies of the missing scouts lay scattered. Sol determined they were killed by apparent strangling, and as Kol inspected the moonwell a large monstruous tangle of vines erupted from the black pool and attacked, whipping a vine in his direction. As the others rushed to help Kol, the monstruous creature, a corrupted [shambling mound](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195203-shambling-mound), began summoning twig blights, swirling black fog, and also trying swallow the fighters.**

**During the melee, Éamon rushed forward and used the power of the moon amulet to cleanse the pool, clearing the corruption and the surrounding black fog. The shambling mound raged at this and wrapped itself around Éamon and Fink, each able to  finally break free, but after swallowing Éamon multiple times, the creature spat the unconscious form of the noble fighter on to the ground. Sol, rushed to stabilize Éamon and Kol provided some healing to help revive their fallen comrade, and then the Lightning Nuts unleashed on the shambling mound, the enraged Fink landing the killing blow.**

**In the aftermath of the battle, the Lightning Nuts bathed in the healing waters of the moonwell, and Kol prayed to the Earthmother, happy to feel her restored connection to the moonwell through the waters. Before departing Al-Dahr made some gurneys so the party could return the bodies of the fallen scouts to Kingsbay, and even in the short hour that they rested, they could see signs of the woods returning back to life. Arriving back in Kingsbay near dusk, the gate guards greatfully took the bodies of their fallen scouts to make arrangements for their services. From there Kol, Sol, and Fink went to report to Lord Aaron, while Al-Dahr and Éamon returned to the Silver Salmon Inn.**

**Lord Aaron was astounded and happy to hear of the Lightning Nuts success in cleansing the moonwell. He rewarded the party with 75GP each and a writ of patronage to Silver Salmon Inn. He also made it know that he would have patrols vigilantly protect the now cleansed moonwell, and asked if they could return in the morning, wanting to discuss to other ways the party could help out in the area. That evening back in the Inn, the five discussed the request and decided to hear Lord Aaron out, but knowing that the moonwell at Darkhorn Castle was the next priority.**

## Chapter 4 - Darkhorn Castle
**In the morning, you visited the market again to pick up more weapons and supplies. An exhausted Al-Dahr also presented, Sol with a new magic item he worked all night, a [dark shard amulet](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228417-dark-shard-amulet). From there you made your way to Lord Aaron's manor to hear his offer, and soon learned that he knew more about your quest then he has previously let on. More importantly, he was aware of your next task, journeying to Darkhorn Castle, and was willing to help.**

**He was able to suggest two possible routes to the castle, one by land and another by sea, and highlighted the risks and advantages of each. After which, he requested you take the land route, using the Old Road, in order to learn as much about the increasing Outcast threat as possible, and if they opportunity presented itself, to deal with the threat. At your request, he sent one of his messenger ravens (which Éamon named North) so that you could relay anything you might learn along the way. He also provided you with two healing potions, before detailing the dark history of the castle.**

**He told a tale of an ancient warlord, King Melvair Darkhorn, who tried to conquer the island of Gwynneth using evil warriors and dark magic. His undoing came when he crossed the mountains to the west, attacking the ancient Llewyrr elves. The elves, with the aid of the Earthmother, fought back using their druidic magic, ending Lord Melvair's reign and killing the warlord in the final battle. In retribution against his use of dark magic, the elves awoke the forest around his castle and used nature's power to destroy the dark castle. Since then the ruins have been a place surrounded by superstition and typically avoided. Recently boats have gone missing in the waters near the old ruins, while locals spread rumors of dark riders, hideous monsters, and undead roaming the lands nearby.**

**Agreeing to take the Old Road north, you set out amidst a light rain and soon found the road overgrown and hard to follow. The march north was slow, and although the first night passed peacefully, Kol and Sol did hear something large moving out in the distance, as fog began to settle in. The next morning was an even slower march, due to the dense fog and just before midday you were ambushed by a group of Outcasts. The bandits may have had the element of surprise, but as you quickly made work of the Outcast Captain, their morale began to waver. One was killed trying to flee and you eventually routed the rest. Searching the bodies, Sol found a map, that Al-Dahr was able to decipher as the location and troop details of a nearby Outcast Encampment within the abandoned town of Thorndyke. He then copied the map, and you sent this back to Kingsbay using North the raven. Moving on you soon took rest again.**

**During the evening, while Éamon was taking the first watch, the others were pulled into a dark dream. Kol, Fink, Al-Dahr, and Sol found themselves transported to an ancient dungeon and heard a whispering coming from a black veil within a skull lined door chanting, "Bhaal awaits thee, Bhaal embraces thee, none escape Bhaal." Moving down steps, deeper into the dungeon, the four were attacked by ghouls and a ghast, with the symbol of Bhaal carved into their foreheads.**

**As the monsters attacked, clawing at biting, a coffin in the back of the dungeon shifted open and a mummy, adorned in rotting black robes, stumbled out. Shuffling forward he afixed his terrifying gaze on Kol, sending the small goblin into a frightened panic, leaving Fink to take the brunt of the attacks. As the ghouls and ghast continued to bite and claw, the mummy began wailing his rotting fists upon the small rage-filled fairy, eventually knocking him unconscious. Kol, facing his fear, leapt forward to heal Fink and the four were able to vanquish the undead monsters. As quiet descended upon the dungeon, the four awoke in the camp, whole and healthy, an amulet of Bhaal (worn by the mummy) mysteriously appearing in the dirt beside them. Kol tried, unsuccessfully, to hit the amulet, before Sol used his mage hand to place it in his pack.**

**With the Outcast's map of the area in hand, you decided to scout out the village of Thorndyke that the bandits were using as an encampment. You attempted to sneak up to the camp, but were soon discovered by a scout who you quickly disposed of and hid in a nearby tree. Wary of other guards, Sol turned Fink invisible and the small barbarian began flying around the camp, identifying the kitchen and the leaders' planning room. Fink overheard plans to sneak into Kingsbay in order to attack the barracks and Lord Aaron's manor, and you decided the best option was to try and stop them. Fink, scouting further, discovered the storeroom, armory, and barracks, where he found six sleeping outcast soldiers. Letting his rage fuel him, he went one by one silently killing each of the soldiers, until finally the last one awoke and he was able silence them before they could raise the alarm. Meanwhile, Al-Dahr entered the kitchen, and disguised as a guard, asked the cook for some food. She graciously gave him two bowls of hearty yet bland stew.**

**Fink's attempt at stealth was all for naught, however, since Éamon, trying to be sneaky, shot a crossbow at another guard, and rather than killing them, just hit them in the leg. The guard immediately began screaming in pain, and the leaders soon rushed to join the fray. The ensuing battle was tense, the leaders seemed to have the upper hand, while Kol, Fink, Sol, and Éamon  struggled to do any meaningful harm (Al-Dahr spending most of his time stealing more food supplies and teleporting around), but eventually (with some healing from Al-Dahr) the Lightning Nuts were able to take out the hardened Outcast leaders. While searching the bodies for "clues" Sol found a note on the Outcast Captain, Gareth, from someone named the Dark Druid. In the letter the Dark Druid invited the outcasts to join them at Darkhorn Castle so they could plan further corruption of the moonwells, mentioning that the phrase "Bhaal awaits thee, Bhaal embraces thee" would provide access to their hideout in the cellars of the ruined castle.**

**Following the map north, the Lightning Nuts soon arrived at the ruins of Darkhorn Castle. Exploring for the entrance to the cellar, they found razor sharp vines clinging to the ruined walls, weakened stone where the ground collapsed under foot, an ancient magical longsword, and the warded entrance to the cellar. Speaking the pass phrase, they were able to safely enter the cellar and soon found themselves face to face with a cultist. Before you could try to decieve the cultist, Éamon sprung to the attack. During the fight, Al-Dahr and Solomon explored the rooms in the cellar, finding most in disrepair, but discovered a storeroom, an armory with worn weapons, and coffins turned cots. Searching the coffins you discovered some gold and silver, along with a black knife with the symbol of Bhaal engraved on it, which Sol took.**

**After the brief fight, you found yourselves in a small chamber with a water well. Inspecting the well (i.e. standing inside it), you discovered that it was only a couple feet deep. Looking around the edges, Solomon discovered a hidden switch that moved the well aside, revealing a tunnel going deeper below the ground. Following it down, you found a large crypt occupied by more cultists, including a corrupted druid, Oishannan. In the fight that ensued, Oishannan raised some of the skeletons from the crypt to aid the cultists, but despite the undead aid, you were able to vanquish the cultists. As Oishannan lay dying, he cackled that you were too late to stop the Dark Druid (his master) but interrogating him further you were only able to learn of a path at the back of the crypt that leads to the Darkhon Moonwell. As you searched for the path, you discovered an ancient tomb, filled with nothing but bone dust and an old necklace. As Éamon reached for the necklace, Sol quickly snagged it first and though much of the necklace dissolved to dust in his hands, a small shard of black stone remained. Closing his hand around the stone, Kol and Fink noticed Sol's eyes go slightly dark at the edges.** 

**After doing some tests, having Sol drop the stone, pass the stone and pick the stone back up, not much changed about the darkness of the edges of his eyes, other than it softening slightly. Afterwards, you found it prudent to rest, during which Sol attuned to the stone while Kol cooked some fortifying treats. Rested, you made your way through the dark narrow tunnel you discovered at the back of the tomb. The path gradually ascended over the next hour until abruptly exiting out a small crevice in a cliffside bordering some woods. In the full dark of night you decided to push forward, and at first found it difficult to navigate, even getting lost from the main path at one point and having to back track. As you neared the corrupted moonwell, however, the familiar signs of death and decay became evident, and you were even confronted by a new menace, zombies raised by the corruption!**

**It did not take long to dispatch of the undead, and soon after you found the corrupted moonwell, this one guarded by more cultists and another corrupted druid. Unable to cleanse the moonwell, due to the failed expenditure of the Moon Amulet by Eamon, you were forced to fight these foes at full strength. The arcane enemies used their magics to hold Eamon in place, while also wielding the power of storms, blight, and conventional weapons. In the end, however, you were able to overcome these enemies and settled down into a long rest, hoping to restore the moonwell in the morning with the recharged amulet.**

## Chapter 5 - The Sacred Grove
**In the morning, the amulet once again hummed with power, and after "linking" the pools with rope, Éamon released the power of the amulet, cleansing the three pools. With the connection to the Earthmother restored, Kol entered one of the pools and heard the Earthmother's thanks and her call for them to come to the Sacred Grove. Then, Kol felt a surge of worry come through the connection and the Earthmother warned that the Dark Druid was attempting to gain entry to the Sacred Grove, then the connection was severed.**

**Panicking, Kol began to spring back down the forest path, the others following closely behind, until finally he realized he didn't know where he was going. Taking a moment to breath, the others calmed the young goblin down, and they decided to scan their maps while Fink flew above the trees to scout the best path towards the Sacred Grove. After a rather lengthy (some might say too long unless they are aware of the concept of choice paralysis) debate, the five decided to try a mountain path that led up towards an old town called Highhome. As they began making their way towards the mountains, they stumbled upon an old eccentric merchant, Tylwyth Teg, sporting an oversized backpack stuffed with items. Tylwyth greeted them cheerily and offered to make a trade. He laid out three items, a wooden box, a monocle, and a black silk handkerchief, and asked for an item with history in return for one of his three. Sol, removed a necklace taken from one of the cultists, and told of how they acquired it. Excited, Tylwyth took the unique item studying it, and claimed it comes from an ancient era when a powerful evil known as Kazgoroth nearly destroyed the Moonshae Isles. He gladly accepted the trade and Sol picked up the black silk handkerchief. The old man then offered some knowledge of Highhome, remarking that the mining town has been deserted for many decades, but a friend of his, Galeb, can help you through the mines as long as you don't touch any of the ore in the mines. He then wish you farewell and disappeared into the woods beyond.** 

**Moving forward you soon found your way to edge of the woods and the mountain path beyond, making your way to Highhome by nightfall. As Tylwyth mentioned, you found a deserted town, inspecting an old mining office with records of operations up until about 60 years ago, a general store with mostly useless items, and finally an old inn and saloon. Scouting the saloon, Fink quickly discovered an aged bottle of whiskey that he pocketed, Kol found a fully furnished kitchen and went to work cooking, Sol and Al-Dahr found some rooms to sleep in, and Éamon found a latrine. Making camp for the night in the saloon, things passed quietly, with the exception of some strange far off noises that Sol heard in the early morning shift.**

**Descending into the mines, you found a vast network of tunnels, following some paths to the dead ends, and needing to blindly choose a direction to take in other cases. After sometime wandering, you seemed to hit another fork in the road, when one of the boulders suddenly came to life and Galeb, a creature made from rock, introduced himself. When hearing that you were on a quest to aid the Earthmother, Galeb instructed you to follow him as he slowly made his way down one of the tunnels. When he hit a dead end, he raised his hands, made a low rumbling sound, and as he did so the rock began to shift, forming into a small cave opening, beyond which was the forest bordering the Sacred Grove.**

**Kol feeling the pull of the Earthmother began navigating the party through the forest. After an hour of travel, the Lightning Nuts stumbled upon a small clearing where a group of worried dryads encircled an injured unicorn. They learned that the Dark Druid had tried to bleed the unicorn for power, and now the animal lay dying. Casting speak with animals, Kol moved slowly towards the creature, promising to heal the black veins of corruption spreading from the wound. Using his connection to the Earthmother, he was able to heal the wounded creature and before it departed, they touched Kol with their horn in a blessing. Shortly after returning to the path in the forest, they found the way changing into a magical entrance to the Sacred Grove.
