# Unseelie Fey of the Moonshae Isles

The Unseelie Fey lean toward darker, more dangerous aspects of the Feywild—trickery, cruelty, decay, and predation. 

## Core Themes

- Corruption of nature (twisted forests, predatory wildlife)
  
- Opposition to the Earthmother (balance vs. decay)

- Hidden courts and ancient pacts
