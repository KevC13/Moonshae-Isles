3. ==**STEWARD OF THE GROVE**==

> [!Read aloud]
> You emerge from the cavern to a dark cloudless night, the stars barely visible due to the brightness of the full moon. Around you is a circle of large standing stones, surrounded by a dense forest. At the edge of the standing stones sits a robed figure by a small campfire. A rich feminine voice, greets you, "You've finally found your way out of the Moonwell, excellent, please join me by the fire, we have much to discuss."

![[1.4 Corwell Grove Stone Circle.png|208]]
- The Steward of the Grove
		1. Immortal form of the Queen and Great Druid [Robyn Kendrick](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Robyn_Kendrick)
		2. DESCRIPTION: Wears a rust colored cloak over a sage green dress. Beneath the hood you can make out a middle-aged woman with bright green eyes and thick dark black hair streaked with a few strands of grey white. She smiles warmly as she speaks.
		3. INFORMATION:
			- Welcome to the Moonshae Isles. 
				-  Does that name many anything to you?
					- Eamon has a faint flicker of familiarity at the name. (HIS DC10)
					- Others (HIS DC13)
			- This place is a sacred site of power known as a moonwell dedicated to the goddess of nature, the [Earthmother](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Earthmother) 
			- The Earthmother brought each of you here, through the power of the Moonwell, to aid her in her time of need. 
			- This is one of many Moonwells, near the city of Caer Corwell. Throughout the land, the Moonwells are being corrupted, weakening the Earthmother and endangering all of Moonshae. 
			- What the Earthmother intends, I do not know.
			- Asked only to greet you, to bring you some needed items and to point you in the proper direction.
				- GUESS: She sees some good in you, some important role you have to play, but that you will need to find your own strength before you can succeed.
				- It is her way. Plant a seed and watch it grow. 
			- Please inspect the items I have brought for you. Once you are ready, I will explain what's next.

4. ==THE WAY FORWARD==
	- Once the PCs have prepared their gear, The Steward of the Grove will invite them back fireside and explain;
		- The Earthmother has retreated to the Sacred Grove as a precaution.
		- The land Sarifal (once known as Gwenneth) is the core of her connection to Moonshae.
		- You must first cleanse the Moonwells at Kingsbay and Darkhorn Castle to stabilize her power. Only then will she be able to open the Sacred Grove and give you answers.
		- There is an old Earthmother temple in the ruined city of Caer Corwell nearby. Beneath the altar is a relic imbued with the power of the moon, by the Earthmother long ago. You will need it to cleanse the Moonwells.
			- Caer Corwell Warning: Unseelie Fey and malicious beasts have inhabited the ruined city since the fall of the Ffolk. Stick to the edges, do not linger, and do not go to the castle. 
			- Kingsbay: The port town is small, but it will offer some respite and a chance to buy anything you may need. It is mainly inhabited by Ffolk, but all kinds are welcome.
			- Myrloch Vale Warning: Beware crossing the mountains into the NW. Since the return and reign of High Lady Ordalf most of Sarifal has been claimed for the leShay (high fey beings). The Llewar moon elves have sworn their allegiance to the High Lady and both groups are not fond of outsiders, particularly humans. Any travels in their lands will need to carry the High Lady's approval.
		- You may rest safely here this evening. In the morning I will show you the way forward.
	- REST: During the evening their dreams will be filled the memories of who they are, with the exception of the last couple of weeks.
