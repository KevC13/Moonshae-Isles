OUTLINE: The party will find themselves on the Isle of Moray, Eamon's home isle. Investigating with learn of the destruction of his former home, the kidnapping of his mother, and the monstrous werewolf tribe, the Black Bloods, that have been pillaging the isles, taking captives. Caer Moray will offer a safe haven, and a place for knowledge, rest and supplies. In addition, Eamon's mentor will be in the town, willing to provide information and acts as a companion on their quest to confront the Black Bloods. They will have to confront the Black Blood Tribe to stop the sacrifices that they are performing and to rescue Eamon's mother.
