==DARKHORN CASTLE==
Characters have to seek out an abandoned fortress atop sea cliffs north of Kingsbay to access the moonwell located in a grove behind the castle. The locals believe it haunted and avoid the surrounding waters entirely.

Truth About Darkhorn Castle
- Used by the cult of Bhaal as a base of operations to defile the moonwells on Sarifal, and plan to amass enough power to take on the Earthmother and the LeShay
- Run by a corrupted druid, known as the Dark Druid
- Recruiting the Outcasts to spread unrest and to gather information and supplies for the cult.
- Outcasts that prove themselves worthy become members of the cult
- The castle is currently guarded by cultists.
- The cults believe that Dark Druid is travelling to the Earthmother's Sacred Grove to try to gain entrance. He will fail to gain access but the go to the Fens of the Fallon to corrupt that moonwell next.

Instead of attacking, you find yourself teleported to the edge of the forest that surrounds the village. From outside you can see the village aflame. There are dozens of the dark creatures like the one you killed mere months ago, and standing amidst them is a tall druid with pale skin, black hair and black eyes. In the light of the fire you can see black veins branching throughout his pale skin. Just before you wake-up, the Dark Druid turns to look at you and smiles.

Normally you might pass the young boy by, but when he looks up your breath catches in your chest. Those eyes, that wild hair, it is so reminiscent of HIM.
