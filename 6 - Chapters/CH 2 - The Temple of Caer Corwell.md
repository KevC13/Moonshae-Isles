1. ==EXITING THE GROVE==

> [!Read Aloud] Read Aloud
> You emerge from the forest beside a small two foot high standing stone with the rose pattern of the Earthmother carved into it. As the fawn steps from the forest it disappears and the path behind you closes.
> Before you lies a great open vale that stretches east as far as the eye can see. To the west the vale ends in a small port city nestled between a high rocky cliff and a large cove. Atop the high cliff stands a large castle. Even from here you can tell the city was once beautiful, but now stands in ruins. Near where the vale meets the city wall you can see a large round temple like structure.

3. ==THE TEMPLE==

> [!Read Aloud]
> The outside of the Temple is dilapidated, like much of the rest of the city. The structure is round with columns supporting an exterior walkway, some broken or missing entirely. An opening to the temple can be seen on the eastside and southside as you approach. 
> 
> Inside the Temple, there is much less natural wear and tear. Brilliant white stone, mithral, and moonstone are all used for elaborate decorations. The center of the roof is open to the sky. Beneath the skylight is a small marble altar sitting at the base of a 20 foot tall marble statue depicting the Earthmother as an elderly woman adorned in clothes of flowing vines, leaves and flowers. Obvious gouges show where something has attempted to vandalize the figure. Around the rest of the rotunda are carved various images related to the Moon, from moon flowers to owls and symbols of the Moon in all its phases.

4. ==TRAVEL TO KINGSBAY==
- ~50 miles from Caer Corwell to Kingsbay 
	- 3 days of traveling at a normal pace (arriving early on day 3)
	- 4 days traveling slowly
	- Each Rest = Learn something new about themselves (STAT or Species FEAT)
	- Track Rations and waterskin each day
		- Waterskins hold 4 pints of water
		- Each PC requires a min of 4 pints/per day to avoid dehydration (1 level of EXH)
			- d20 = -2 per EXH level
			- speed = -5 feet per EXH level
		- Must drink 8 pints (1 gal) to recover from dehydration 
- 2 SURVIVAL checks per day: (FAIL = Extra 1/3 day of travel)
	- Day 1 - Following an old road - DC10
		- Allow time for PC communication
		- After midday d20 check 
			- Wind shifts blowing from the west (behind them)
			- Sol hear the faint sound of pursuit
			- Goblin/Sprite encounter
		- Evening: stop for rest, eat 1 ration, drink the last of their water.
		- Rain overnight
	- **DREAM = MEMORY GAIN**
		- 
	- Day 2 - Road overgrown and disappeared giving way to grassy plain - DC16
		- The road begins to fade over the course of the first few hours of travel
			- 1st Check: DC13
			- 2nd + Check: DC16
			- SEARCHING FOR WATER: 
				- Slow pace (required extra check to reach O'Malley)
				- DC13 = Find a small pond on the travels
					- Purify spell (Al-Dahr and Kol)
					- CON13 check to ingest without getting sick
