OUTLINE: The party will be thrown into the Dark Between moonwells, the connection allowing direct travel having been severed by the corruption via Kazgoroth's heart. They must navigate the Dark, surviving the perils and the lack of resources.

1. ==Dark Between==


> [!NOTE] Read Aloud
> You find yourself in a foreign realm complete surrounded by darkness. The ground appears to be an obsidian-like black glass and in the sky above hangs a slightly less black orb, providing what little light there is in this space. 

- Magical Sources of Light
	- Draw creatures of the Dark Between
		- 1st: Raven-like creatures that are made of and fly by swirling shadows
			- Feed on the magical light
		- 2nd: Shadows
		- 3rd: Juvenile Shadow Dragon
- Fink's Beacon
	- Being connected to the Shadowfell, Fink will feel a pulse-like pull just off the direction they had been heading. 
		- Taking 10 minutes to sit quietly to feel the pulse = know what direction to head
			- INSIGHT check to determine this
		- ADV on Survival to follow the pull - DC12
	- Take 3 days of successful travel to see the "beacon"
		- Day 1: if 1/2 ration of water = DC15 CON or gain level EXH
		- Day 2: No water = 1 level of EXH
		- Day 3: No water = 1 level of EXH
	- Take 2 more days travelling slowly to reach the Beacon
		- Day 4: No water = 1 level of EXH
		- Day 5: No water = 1 level of EXH (max of 4th or 5th level)


> [!NOTE] Read Aloud
> You arrive at a bright moonlight colored orb, roughly 5 feet in diameter. Two bright thin lines of light extend from the orb, fading far off into the distance, but a broken line of light extends only a few feet in the direction from which you are walking. 

- Fink will feel the pulsing coming strongly from the orb. 
	- If he makes contact = Sense the ability to open a temporary portal to the other side
- DC14 SURVIVAL = knows the other lines lead North and Northeast
- DC14 ARCANA = knows the lines lead towards other moonwells, portal connections
