==1. Overview of Kingsbay==
Kingsbay is a hardy coastal settlement of roughly 700 residents nestled between rocky cliffs and dark pine forests. The town survives on fishing, whaling, peat harvesting, and trade with passing ships.
The Ffolk of Kingsbay are deeply loyal once trust is earned. Tales of spirits, fey curses, and ancient druidic magic are common here — and lately, fear has begun to spread.
	•	Encroaching corruption - The Kingsbay Moonwell	
	•	Haunted ruins - Darkhorn Castle
	•	Political tension - Ffolk vs. LeShay, Cult vs. Earthmother

==2. Important Locations==
**1. The Silver Salmon Inn**
The Silver Salmon Inn is the social heart of Kingsbay.
A sturdy two-story inn with driftwood beams, whale-oil lanterns, and peat fires burning at all hours. Locals gather downstairs in the tavern nightly for ale, music, gambling, and rumors.
Atmosphere
	•	Warm but noisy
	•	Sea shanties, fiddle music, stories, the Bards of Moonshae 
	•	Walls decorated with harpoons and massive fish skeletons
	•	Private back room used for discreet meetings

Owner: Maeve Thorne
![3.3 Maeve Thorne|132](<../4 - Images/3.3 Maeve Thorne.png>)
Female Human, middle-aged, sharp-eyed and practical
Maeve is a former sailor who inherited the inn after her husband disappeared at sea fifteen years ago.
Personality
	•	Wry humor
	•	Distrustful of nobles
	•	Protective of townsfolk
	•	Notices everything, quietly gathering info from travelers and sailors.
What Maeve Knows
	•	Several fishermen recently vanished near Darkhorn Point. Boats won't travel there anymore.
	•	Black tides, dead fish appearing in the waters to the south
	•	Bandits/Outcasts have been roaming and raiding the east coast.
			- Bear the Outcast mark (triangle with a slash through it)
			- Mark = Branded by the Leshay as outcasts
			- Not accepted within Kingsbay or other settlements on Sarifal for fear of LeShay reprisal
			- Earned for trespassing in the Myrloch Vale 
			- Stricter violations (poaching, stealing, spying, etc) = death
	•	A cloaked druid (the Steward) visited Kingsbay weeks ago and warned: “The Moonwell weeps black water.”
Quest Hook: Trouble on the Seas is Bad for Business
- Maeve asks the party to investigate a missing fishing crew and/or waters to the south
- Secret: Maeve’s husband may still be alive — transformed into something monstrous by the cult in Darkhorn.

**2. The Lord's Lodge - Kingsbay Hall**
Laird Aaron MacFeagus
![3.4 Laird Aaron MacFaergus|198](<../4 - Images/3.4 Laird Aaron MacFaergus.png>)
Male Ffolk Noble, aging but formidable
The ruler of Kingsbay and protector of the surrounding lands. Aaron rules from Kingsbay Hall, a large lodge in the heart of Kingsbay.
He descends from ancient Ffolk nobility and maintains old alliances with druids of the Earthmother.

Personality
	•	Stern but honorable
	•	Exhausted from sleepless nights
	•	Burdened and worried by the darkness spreading

What Aaron Knows
- If the party earns his trust, he reveals:
Aaron is one of the few who understands the true danger threatening Kingsbay.
- Met with the Steward of the Grove two weeks back
Quest: The Corrupted Moonwell
- Hidden within an ancient grove south of town lies the Kingsbay Moonwell, once sacred to the Earthmother.
Over the last month:
	•	The water has darkened
	•	Animals near the grove become violent
	•	Dark creatures attack those that enter the woods
	•	Bandits/Outcasts plague nearby settlements
Aaron Learned:
	•	Someone has intentionally corrupted the Moonwell
	•	The corruption may spread across Sarifal or all of Moonshae
	•	Darkhorn Castle is somehow connected
		- Ancient castle that predates many of the Moonshae Kingdoms
		- History is largely ancient Ffolk tales
		- Evil once tried to spread from the land in the ancient days of the Llewar elves. The first Ffolk banded with the Llewar to stop the evil, and now the place is said to be cursed. 
	- He has secretly sent scouts to investigate the Moonwell — none returned.
What Aaron Asks of the Party
	•	Investigate the Moonwell
		•	Discover/stop the source of the corruption
		- Offers 250GP, noble favor, and will cover lodging at the Silver Salmon Inn


**3. Market Square**
A muddy central commons surrounded by smithies, merchants, and tradesmen.
Notable NPCs
	•	Morna Reedcloak — herbalist with minor druidic knowledge
	•	Durgan Pike — blacksmith who once served in Caer Callidyrr
	•	Brother Talien — traveling priest increasingly disturbed by local omens


**4. The Harbor and Smokehouse**
A crowded dock lined with fishing vessels, crab traps, and weather-beaten sailors.
Features
	•	Constant smell of salt, tar, and smoked fish
	•	Fishermen whisper of “black tides” appearing offshore to the south
	•	Several boats refuse to fish near Darkhorn Point
Encounters
	•	Drunken sailors brawl nightly
	•	Strange dead fish wash ashore with pale white eyes
	•	PCs may spot cloaked figures unloading crates after midnight (cult of Bhaal activity)

==3. The Kingsbay Moonwell==
A sacred spring hidden within a stone circle deep in the forest.
Current State
Once shimmering silver-blue, the waters now swirl with oily black veins.
Effects Nearby
	•	Whispering voices at night
	•	Sick livestock
	•	Twisted wildlife
	•	Cold fog rolling through the trees
	•	Fey crossings destabilizing
Creatures Near the Moonwell
Suitable for Levels 3–5:
	•	Corrupted wolves
	•	Twig blights
	•	Shadow-touched boars
	•	Bog wraiths
	•	A maddened satyr druid

Darkhorn Castle
An abandoned fortress atop sea cliffs west of Kingsbay.
The locals call it haunted and avoid the surrounding moors entirely.

Rumors About Darkhorn Castle
Roll or choose:
d8	Rumor
1	Green lights flicker in the castle windows during storms.
2	A headless rider patrols the old road at midnight.
3	Fishermen hear screaming from beneath the cliffs.
4	The castle belonged to a lord who bargained with dark fey.
5	Children vanish if they wander too close to Darkhorn Moor.
6	Secret tunnels connect the castle to ancient druid catacombs.
7	A monstrous black stag guards the castle gates.
8	The dead do not remain buried near Darkhorn Castle.
Truth About Darkhorn Castle
Much of the haunting is real.
Beneath the castle lies:
	•	An ancient fey crossing
	•	Forgotten druidic tombs
	•	A sealed shadow entity slowly awakening
A cult dedicated to corrupted fey powers has recently occupied portions of the ruins.

Adventure Hooks
1. The Black Tide
Dead fish and bloated sea creatures wash ashore carrying traces of Moonwell corruption.
2. Missing Scouts
Aaron’s men vanished investigating Darkhorn Castle.
3. The Weeping Grove
A druid begs the party to cleanse the Moonwell before the next full moon.
4. The Innkeeper’s Secret
Maeve receives a message she believes came from her lost husband.
5. Shadows in the Fog
Villagers disappear whenever the unnatural sea fog rolls in.

Tone & Style Notes
Kingsbay works best when played as:
	•	Grim but hopeful
	•	Superstitious and isolated
	•	Rich with ancient lore
	•	Slowly escalating cosmic dread
The town should feel like a place standing at the edge of something ancient awakening beneath the Isles.
