- AL-DAHR:
	- "Al-Dahr there are more this time and they are coming close. PLEASE HE..." Something slams into the door, cutting the man's word short, prompting you to action. You reach for the dark shard amulet you have been trying to develop and try to cast an attack spell at the door, but it backfires. Instead of attacking, you find yourself teleported to the edge of the forest that surrounds the village. From outside you can see the village aflame. There are dozens of the dark creatures like the one you killed mere months ago, and standing amidst them is a tall druid with pale skin, black hair and black eyes. In the light of the fire you can see black veins branching throughout his pale skin. As you stare at him, the Dark Druid turns to look at you and smiles...

Looking up you see the Dark Druid stalk slowly in your direction, and your sense of fear strengthens. You try the amulet again but it shatters in your hand as you try to focus your magic. Then he there before you, the shadows seeming to shift around him. He grabs hold of your head and and wrenches your eyes towards your burning village. "You thought to defy me and my creations," he whispers in your ear, "now watch as the fires consume your people and herald the return of Kazgoroth." Then you feel a hot tearing pain plunge through your back, a dark blade emerging from your chest. As you life drains out the last thing you see is the Dark Druid stalking back towards the flaming ruins of your people, then death takes you.
- ÉAMON
	- You remember training late into the evening, when a cry of alarm arose from the town just outside the castle walls. Running up to the battlements, you arrived in time to see several men within the village walls transforming in to monstrous wolflike shapes. Several of the creatures rushed to open the gates, while the others began ravaging the villagers unlucky enough to be out in the streets this night. Beyond the gates you see, you sees dozens of torches come alight as a wave of monstrous creatures of all forms come rushing towards the gates. A loud horn sounds the call to arms in the village. "Finally my time has come" is the thought that springs to mind as you rush to meet the martial force amassing in the castle yard. As you reach the ground, your father walks towards you with a hand up. "Son, stay here. If we fail, you are needed to protect our home and your mother." With that he rushes out towards town with his guards on his heels, while you are left disappointed...
	- ...It is agonizing hearing the sounds of battle beyond the walls. Just as your resolve breaks and you begin to rush towards the gates, a cry goes up and the gates begin to creak open. Your father stumbles forward, slashes marks made from massive claws mar his entire body. He turns to close the gates, but before he can secure them they crash open and a huge black werewolf with a silver scar through one eye stalks forward, flanked by two smaller werewolves. The black werewolf grabs your shocked father by the throat, lifting him into the air and with a quick slash claws decapitates him. 
	- Then there is a stampede of smaller wolflike men wielding axes and short swords flooding into the keep. A reckless fire rises inside you and you draw your sword rushing the crowd, trying to cut through the tide towards the black werewolf. You cut through 5, 10, 15 and then the tide overwhelms you. Axes and swords meet flesh, slashing and piercing your arms, legs and torso. You fall to your knees, and then begin to drift sideways towards the muddy ground. The last thing you see as death takes you is your mother being dragged from the keep and the thought of failure rising towards your mind.

Carefully making your way forward, you found a few highwaymen threatening an elderly couple travelling on the road. The old man was shakily holding out a sword, desperately trying to protect his wife. The bandits toyed with the elderly couple, but when the old man scored a hit on one of the bandits they turned murderous. You could not stand by and watch. You drew your small herb reaping sickle and before the lead bandit could strike down the old man you summoned a strength you did not know you possessed and swung. Rather than being cowed, you are lifted by the knowledge that you are protecting others who cannot protect themselves. You raise your sickle and begin to rush the other two, but you only make it a few short steps before pain erupts in your stomach and looking down you see an crossbow bolt protruding from your belly. One of the bandits loosed a bolt...

You're breathing becomes difficult and as you begin to fall you here the sound of rustling brush and see the hero, who saved before rush into stop the bandits and finish what you have started.
