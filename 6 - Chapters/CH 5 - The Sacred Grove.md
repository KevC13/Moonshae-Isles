==DREAM = MEMORY GAIN==
- SOL:
	- You are back at the mouth of the coastal cavern, feeling the pull towards the darkness. The pull is stronger now, and you remember stepping through the ankle high water into the darkness of the cavern. As your eyes adjust you see a pure white marble altar gleaming in the back of the cavern. A snowflake design surround by a diamond shape outline has been chiseled into the face of the altar. Sitting atop the altar is a mirror black stone the size of your fist. It is towards this stone that you are being pulled...
	- As the stone calls you deeper in to the cavern, you feel overwhelmed by the strength of the pull. Your legs move of their own accord even as your begin to wade deeper into a pool of water between you and the altar, first knee deep, then chest deep, until it is lapping at your chin. The closer your step, the clearer you can here a voice in your head urging you forward, "Yes, nearer! Claim the stone and become my champion!" As you draw closer, beginning to rise towards the dry land of the altar, some part of you is uneasy, fearing the unknown dark stone ahead, but the voice overrides your doubt, forcing you forward. Finally, you reach the altar and as your shaky hand reaches towards the stone you feel something begin to wrap around your legs, arms and torso, holding you in place. Looking down you see green vines rising from the water, and despite fighting to free yourself, you do not have the strength to break them. You feel the vines begin to turn you around, and as you rotate a tall fey elf adorned in green leaf shaped armor and wielding a large hook shaped blade walks towards you on the water. "So this is the newest minion of the Queen of Air and Darkness. I see you are not yet bound by her power, but..." As he draws nearer, you are lifted in his direction by the vines until you meet eye to eye, "...it seems she has already wheedled her way into your mind. I can see her there behind your eyes. That is a pity. The Summer Queen can be forgiving, but she cannot allow her sister to break free of her prison. It seems the shard must be moved, and though I regret it, you must not be allowed to gain her power." With that, the vines begin to pull you into the dark pool of water. You struggle again in vain against the restraints, but to no avail. As your lungs burn hotter and hotter for air, you finally succumb, sucking in water, and slip into the darkness of death.
- AL-DAHR:
	- "Al-Dahr there are more this time and they are coming close. PLEASE HEL..." Something slams into the door, cutting the man's word short, prompting you to action. You reach for the dark shard amulet you have been trying to develop and try to cast an attack spell at the door, but it backfires. Instead of attacking, you find yourself teleported to the edge of the forest that surrounds the village. From outside you can see the village aflame. There are dozens of the dark creatures like the one you killed mere months ago, and standing amidst them is a tall druid with pale skin, black hair and black eyes. In the light of the fire you can see black veins branching throughout his pale skin. As you stare at him, the Dark Druid turns to look at you and smiles...
	- ...you then begin to feel vines writhe up your leg, locking you in place. In panic you try to break free, but cannot. Looking up you see the Dark Druid stalk slowly in your direction, and your sense of fear strengthens. You try the amulet again but it shatters in your hand as you try to focus your magic. Then he is there before you, the shadows seeming to shift around him. He grabs hold of your head and and wrenches your eyes towards your burning village. "You thought to defy me and my creations," he whispers in your ear, "now watch as the fires consume your people and herald the return of Kazgoroth." Then you feel a hot tearing pain plunge through your back, a dark blade emerging from your chest. As you life drains out the last thing you see is the Dark Druid stalking back towards the flaming ruins of your people, then death takes you.
- ÉAMON
	- You remember training late into the evening, when a cry of alarm arose from the town just outside the castle walls. Running up to the battlements, you arrived in time to see several men within the village walls transforming in to monstrous wolflike shapes. Several of the creatures rushed to open the gates, while the others began ravaging the villagers unlucky enough to be out in the streets this night. Beyond the gates you see, you sees dozens of torches come alight as a wave of monstrous creatures of all forms come rushing towards the gates. A loud horn sounds the call to arms in the village. "Finally my time has come" is the thought that springs to mind as you rush to meet the martial force amassing in the castle yard. As you reach the ground, your father walks towards you with a hand up. "Son, stay here. If we fail, you are needed to protect our home and your mother." With that he rushes out towards town with his guards on his heels, while you are left disappointed...
	- ...It is agonizing hearing the sounds of battle beyond the walls. Just as your resolve breaks and you begin to rush towards the gates, a cry goes up and the gates begin to creak open. Your father stumbles forward, slash marks made from massive claws mar his entire body. He turns to close the gates, but before he can secure them they crash open and a huge black werewolf with a silver scar through one eye stalks forward, flanked by two smaller werewolves. The black werewolf grabs your shocked father by the throat, lifting him into the air and with a quick slash of sharp claws decapitates him. 
	- Your scream is lost in the sound of a stampede of smaller wolflike men wielding axes and short swords flooding into the keep. A reckless fire rises inside you and you draw your sword rushing the crowd, trying to cut through the tide towards the black werewolf. You cut through 5, 10, 15 and then the tide overwhelms you. Axes and swords meet flesh, slashing and piercing your arms, legs and torso. You fall to your knees, and then begin to drift sideways towards the muddy ground. The last thing you see as death takes you is your mother being dragged from the keep and the thought of failure rising towards your mind. 
- KOL
	- You remembering venturing out in the woods, once again collecting herbs, when you over heard a commotion up head. Carefully making your way forward, you found a few highwaymen threatening an elderly couple travelling on the road. The old man was shakily holding out a sword, desperately trying to protect his wife. The bandits toyed with the elderly couple, but when the old man scored a hit on one of the bandits they turned murderous. You could not stand by and watch. You drew your small herb reaping sickle and before the lead bandit could strike down the old man you summoned a strength you did not know you possessed and swung. Your blade met flesh and you watched as the sword hand of the lead bandit went flying...
	- ...As the lead bandit screams staring at the bleeding stump of his hand, all goes momentarily quiet. Then the other bandits turn their attention towards you. Rather than being cowed, you are lifted by the knowledge that you are protecting others who cannot protect themselves. You raise your sickle and begin to rush the other two, but you only make it a few short steps before pain erupts in your stomach and looking down you see a crossbow bolt protruding from your belly. One of the bandits, lowers his short crossbow and begins to draw a scimitar. 
	- Shaken, you still try to progress forward, just as another bolt from the other bandit takes you in the chest. You're breathing becomes difficult and as you begin to fall you hear the sound of rustling brush and see the hero, who saved you before rush into stop the bandits, finishing what you have started. In the last few moments as your strength begins to fade, the hero comes to your side. "You have done well my brave little one. You are a true protector of the innocent." Then in a much quieter, prayerful tone you here him whisper, "Earthmother, take this one into your embrace." Then all goes black. 
- FINK
	- Its been years since you and Peter parted ways, all because of her. You remember finally settling down in a small city. Its only been a couple of weeks, but its the longest you have stayed anywhere since leaving him. Departing the local tavern one evening, you stumble upon a street urchin. Normally you might pass the young boy by, but when he looks up your breath catches in your chest. Those eyes, that wild hair, it is so reminiscent of HIM. Drawn to the child, you give him a coin and some left over bread you were saving and then force yourself to continue home. Over the next couple of weeks, you continually keep an eye out for the boy, helping when you can. Then one evening, you are stumbling your way home yet again when you find the boy surrounded by three dark figures in hooded cloaks. One has a knife to the boys throat, while another asks him if he has seen someone...someone who sounds exactly like you...
	- ...For a moment you are tempted to leave, but your stubborn protective instincts take over and you begin flying, heedless of the danger, towards the three hooded figures. Rage begins boiling in your veins as you fly for the one with a knife to the boy's throat. You reach for your dagger and plunge it into his side before he can react. Blessedly, the boy runs free, but the man holding him isn't finished yet. He holds the knife in his side and yells at the others, "That's him! Get the fairy fuck!!" That enrages you further, but as you reach for your battle axe you realize that in your drunken exit from the bar you must have left it. You go to retrieve your knife from the side of the one you stabbed, but your momentary hesitation costs you, and a large fist slams you towards the ground. You try to rise but the other two figures are already there, kicking, punching, and slashing towards you. Its like an eternity before the beating stops, then the man you stabbed walks towards your beaten, bloodied and nearly unconscious form. He picks you up, pulls the knife from his side and utters the words, "Tinkerbell sends her regards", just before he plunges the knife through your heart.
