OUTLINE: The party must journey towards and through the Fens of Fallon to confront the Dark Druid and stop the corruption. This time they will find themselves outmatch, and be unsuccessful in stopping the Dark Druid. Instead they will be portaled to a dark realm between worlds that will provide transport between moonwell "gates". 

0. ==CAMPFIRE==
- This is first evening after meeting the Earthmother and learning of their deaths. Around the campfire that night offers them each the chance to share what they have learned or to keep their memory to themselves. 

1. ==FOOTHILLS==

	- After travelling a half day towards the Fens, the will encounter the troll.
		-  MAKE PERCEPTION CHECKS


> [!Read Aloud] Read Aloud
> You hear a loud rumbling snoring sound coming from a hill ahead

- STEALTH = DC14 or the troll will smell the food they carry.

- IF TROLL AWAKES: 
	- Smells food
	- Wants food
	- Fights to get food
	- Retreats if he is reduced to 30HP or less

2. ==FENS (SWAMP)==

- Towards evening during their first day of travel. They will exit the small woods between the foothills and the Fens. 


> [!NOTE] Read Aloud
> You come to an abrupt end of the small woods as dusk descends. Before you lies a large open expanse of wetlands. The terrain is a mix of grassy land masses, reeds, pools of water, and spongy peat layers.

- At Night
	- STANDING GUARD = Towards midnight, see eerie dancing lights off in the distance.
	- TRAVERSING = DC20 SURVIVAL to navigate safely
		- Fens will become more bog like as they near the far shore
		- smell of rot will begin to hand heavy in the air.
			- DC13 CON save or become POISONED until they leave the Fens
		- WILL O' WISP ENCOUNTER

- By Day
	- TRAVERSING = DC15 SURVIVAL to navigate safely
		- See the cloud of rot hanging over the Fens as they near the far shore
		- Fens will become more bog like as they near the far shore
		- Entering the fog = DISADV on SURVIVAL
		- smell of rot will begin to hand heavy in the air.
			- DC13 CON save or become POISONED until they leave the Fens
		- BULLYWUG ENCOUNTER
			- Will hear a soft croaking as they approach the section of swamp
			- The Bog Sage will jump up
				- "I am Lord of the Muddy Pool. Who trespasses upon my swamp?"
				- "Pay the toll"
				- He wants some trinket that will be a visible symbol of his power.
					- DC will be higher for worthless gear vs. lower for valuable gear
			- As the party debates they will hear more croaking begin to rise around them.

3. ==FENS FOREST==

	ARRIVING
	- Depending on how well they travel the Fens, they will either reach the other side by early evening or late in the night. 
		- If the have to camp in the Fens = lower Will O' Wisp encounter
	- Camping in or near forest;
		- Late night guard will hear wooden chimes, rustling in the canopy tops, and whispering that could be the wind. 
	AFTER A COUPLE HOURS
	- The forest begins to thicken with little light passing between the canopy tops.
		- DC13 PERCEPTION
			- Hear the same wooden chimes, rustling and whispering
	- DUSKLING ENCOUNTER
		- If they stay on guard, then normal Initiative
		- If move forward blindly = Advantage to the Dusklings

4. ==CORRUPTED FOREST==


> [!NOTE] Read Aloud
> The air in the dense forest begins to press in heavy on your lungs, and familiar signs of corruption become evident. Decaying trees, thick webs in the canopies, spongy earth underfoot, and dark twisting slick black vines appear as you travel deeper. A wave of nausea overwhelms you as the thick air and dark magic assaults your mind and senses. 

DC13 CON or WIS SAVE or become POISONED
