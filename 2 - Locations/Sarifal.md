### Opening Scene

- The party awakens around a glowing **moonwell**.
    
- A vision of the Earthmother speaks through wind, water, or a white stag.
    
- She warns:
    
    - Moonwells are **weakening**
        
    - Something is **poisoning Sarifal from within the Feywild**
        

### Immediate Conflict

- Corrupted beasts (blights, wolves, twisted fey) attack the grove.
    
- A druid guardian is dying or already dead.
    

### Key NPCs

- **Druid of Corwell Grove** (mentor or fallen guide)
    
- A mysterious **eladrin observer** who vanishes after watching the party
    

### Goals

- Cleanse the local corruption
    
- Learn of **Sarifal’s failing moonwells**
    
- Receive a **moon-touched charm** (plot item)

# 🌟 Resolution

### If Players Succeed:

- Moonwell network stabilizes
    
- Earthmother grants:
    
    - Blessings (boons, feats, magic items)
        
- Sarifal begins to heal
    

### If They Fail (or partially succeed):

- Sarifal becomes unstable
    
- Fey incursions increase
    
- Sets up a **Level 6+ continuation**
