As of the late 15th century DR, were twenty-six known moonwells scattered across the Moonshae Isles, the vast majority of which were conc entrated on the Isle of Gwynneth and particularly within the Myrloch Vale. Each isle had a central moonwell that was particularly important, however moonwells on isles controlled by the [Northmen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Northmen "Northmen") tended to fall into disrepair without [Ffolk](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ffolk "Ffolk") druids to tend them. No moonwells remained on the island of [Norland](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Norland "Norland"). The most notable moonwells included:

- The moonwell of [Corwell](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Corwell "Corwell") grove.
- The moonwell in the [Fens of the Fallen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fens_of_the_Fallen "Fens of the Fallen") in Myrloch Vale.
- The moonwell on [Grayrock](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Grayrock "Grayrock"), which made the barren island fertile and habitable.
- The moonwell in the [Sacred Grove of the Earthmother](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sacred_Grove_of_the_Earthmother "Sacred Grove of the Earthmother") in the east of Myrloch Vale.
- The [moonwell](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Oman_Moonwell "Oman Moonwell") in the [Old Stone Wood](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Old_Stone_Wood "Old Stone Wood") on Oman's Isle.
- The moonwell in [Shannyth Forest](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shannyth_Forest "Shannyth Forest") on Moray.
- 
There also existed a dark moonwell within Creirwy Glade of the Shiverpine Forest in the [Land Under Eternal Ice](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Land_Under_Eternal_Ice "Land Under Eternal Ice"), the [divine domain](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Divine_domain "Divine domain") of the goddess [Auril](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Auril "Auril"). This moonwell was associated with Auril and the [Queen of Air and Darkness](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Queen_of_Air_and_Darkness "Queen of Air and Darkness") rather than with the Earthmother, and connected the domain to the Feywild.
